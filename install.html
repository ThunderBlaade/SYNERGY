Train with friends. Scan to open the app.

Open Synergy

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Private app for friends. You sign in with the email and password you were given.
